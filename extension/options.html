Tip: right-click any page or link and pick Send this site to.

Categories

Each category becomes a tab group. Domain matches always win, and the most specific domain wins; keyword matches follow the order below.

  • domains: a site and its subdomains (gouv.fr matches impots.gouv.fr)
  • hostKeywords: text found in the hostname (moodle)
  • urlKeywords: text found in the full address (/checkout)
  • color: grey, blue, red, yellow, green, pink, purple, cyan, orange

Never group

One domain per line. These sites stay out of every group, even if a keyword matches.
